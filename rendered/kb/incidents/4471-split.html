The three problems

  • parser: the shared parser read every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so “4 March” came out as “3 April”. These came in by app and email, and by form from tenants on booking-v2, whose hint is right. Fixed 5 October: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  • hint: booking-v3 printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People typed what the hint told them, and the form read it day first. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants; the parser fix did not touch it. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, and the hint with it.
  • one-day-move: the date moves by one day, not a swap: Tuesday becomes Monday, a client in Lisbon and one in Toronto get different days for the same meeting, an invite arrives before the booking. A month-first parser cannot do that. It points to time zones. The desk noted these reports in desk-notes.json and files them under parser by tenant, so the parser fix closed them on paper. Still open.

Why one ticket hid them

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject line. All three problems produce the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Dates mixed up”), so they landed in one ticket.
  • Intake’s hint tag follows the reporter’s browser, not the tenant. It marks US-English browsers as hint, and misses real hint reports from local browsers. Count by tenant and channel (causes.ts), never by tag.
  • The export repeats reports across its pages. Count each id once (uniqueById).

What to do now

  1. Do not answer new one-day-move reports with “fixed”. Keep them on their own ticket until someone finds the time-zone cause.
  2. Replace decision 0001 with a new record: merge on cause, not on subject.
